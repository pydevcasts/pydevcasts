# 👋 Hi, I'm a Machine Learning Researcher in Medical Data Science

I build **leakage-aware, interpretable machine learning pipelines** for clinical and multi-omics data, with a focus on **oncology**. I hold an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence & Robotics (GPA 19.27/20, ≈ 3.9/4.0), and I bring **more than 11 years of software engineering experience** into research, so my pipelines are not only scientifically sound but also reusable, testable, and well documented.

---

## 🔬 Research Interests

- **Statistical Machine Learning:** model evaluation, nested cross-validation, leakage control
- **Multi-omics Integration:** late fusion of clinical, transcriptomic, and genomic data
- **Interpretable ML in Oncology:** SHAP, feature stability, biomarker discovery
- **External Validation:** cross-cohort transfer, calibration, generalizability
